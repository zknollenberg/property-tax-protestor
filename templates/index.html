5 How to File Your Protest

  • Deadline May 15 of the appraisal year, or 30 days after CCAD mails your Notice of Appraised Value — whichever is later. Never miss this date.
  • File online (fastest) Visit collincad.org/eprotest to submit your protest electronically. You will need your CCAD account number (shown above).
  • File by mail Mail the printed protest letter above (signed) to:
    Collin Central Appraisal District, 250 W. Eldorado Pkwy, McKinney, TX 75069
  • Informal hearing A CCAD appraiser will contact you to review your evidence. Present the comparable-properties table this app generated. Many cases are resolved here with a value reduction.
  • ARB formal hearing (if needed) If the informal hearing doesn't resolve the dispute, your case goes before the Appraisal Review Board. Submit your comparables evidence at least 14 days before the hearing date.
  • Questions? Call CCAD at (469) 742-9200 or visit collincad.org.
